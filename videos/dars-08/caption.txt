0 dan ingliz tili, 8-dars: inglizcha «I'm not, He isn't, They aren't».

I'm not a doctor — Men shifokor emasman
He isn't happy — U xursand emas
They aren't students — Ular talaba emas

not = emas. isn't = is not, aren't = are not (qisqa shakllari).

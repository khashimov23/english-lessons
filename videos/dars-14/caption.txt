One book, two books — Bitta kitob, ikkita kitob
One pen, three pens — Bitta ruchka, uchta ruchka
One apple, five apples — Bitta olma, beshta olma

Narsa bittadan ko'p bo'lsa, so'z oxiriga -s qo'shiladi: book → books, pen → pens. O'zbekchada «ikkita kitob» deymiz, inglizchada esa «two books».
Saqlab qo'ying va ingliz tilini boshlamoqchi bo'lgan do'stingizga yuboring.
Ertaga 15-dars: Mening, sening.
